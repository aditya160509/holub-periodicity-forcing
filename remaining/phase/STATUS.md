# Holub periodicity-forcing problem: new phase-comparison theorems

## Result and scope

This is a continuation of the supplied `holub-dbound-pack.zip`, specifically its request for a length-difference reduction or direct proofs for whole slices. All new proof claims below are candidate analytical theorems pending independent proof review. The original manuscript's periodic-morphism reduction, balanced-prefix theorem and seed (1,0,1,0) are used as dependencies, not claimed as new.

For words over an arbitrary alphabet consider

$$X^iY^2X^kYX^\ell=U^iV^2U^kVU^\ell,$$

where $k\ge1$ and $i+\ell\ge1$. A tuple forces periodicity if every solution has identical substitutions ($X=U$, $Y=V$) or all four words are powers of one word. Holub's admissibility condition is $|V|\ge\max(|X|,|Y|,|U|)$.

**New main theorem (unrestricted).** The following entire regions force periodicity, without imposing the admissibility condition:

1. $\ell=0$, $i,k\ge1$, $k\ne 2i$.
2. $i=0$, $\ell\ge1$, $k>2\ell$.
3. $i,k,\ell\ge1$, $k\ge2$, $i+k>2\ell$, $i<2(k+\ell)$.

The zero-tail exception $k=2i$ is genuinely nonforcing, including under Holub's length condition, by the supplied family's D explicit witnesses. In particular the large-middle-block slice $k>2(i+\ell)$ is entirely settled. The third region encompasses every strictly nested middle block with positive outer exponents and $k\ge2$. The proof section subdivides this region into overlapping subtheorems; the simplified statement above has the same scope.

The proof is independent of the magnitudes of the word lengths. There is no cutoff on $d=|X|-|U|$. This replaces a difficult finite-bound approach for these families with a direct argument.

## Main mathematical mechanism

Orient the two substitutions so $d=x-u>0$ and let $D=v-y=(i+k+\ell)d/3$. Let $X=R^t$ with primitive $R$ of length $r$. Put $L=2D-id$ and $Q=D-\ell d$. When both are nonnegative, the middle $U^k$ lies inside $X^k$. Factor

$$X^i=U^iC,\qquad X^\ell=BU^\ell,\qquad X^k=PU^kA.$$

The key exact identity is

$$ V=AYB,\qquad CY^2P=(AYB)^2. $$

Thus $C,A$ are prefix-comparable and $P,B$ suffix-comparable. Extending the comparisons by the common $U$ runs and applying uniqueness of primitive rotations gives congruences for $H=Q-id$ and $F=L-\ell d$. Since $F-H=D$, both congruences imply $r\mid D$. This aligns the separate $U$ runs in the same periodic word, forces $U$ to be an $R$-power, and then forces $Y,V$ to be $R$-powers by the two-word code property.

In unbalanced regions only one phase congruence is available. Fine–Wilf and divisor bounds often suffice; the harder regimes use literal cancellation, transport through powers of $R$, and an exponent-count homomorphism on the free subgroup generated by $R,U$. If those two words fail to commute, their independent exponent counts contradict the equation. The short exceptional regimes are excluded using cyclic Hamming-distance invariance, including the zero-head case $(i,k,\ell)=(0,4,1)$.

The complete proofs are in `proof.tex`, written as a Section 10 insertion for the original manuscript. The Markdown report includes a text conversion of that section. The supplied original Sections 1–9 are not re-proved in this continuation.

## What is still open here

The complete Holub classification is **not** solved. No linear or polynomial global bound on d was found. The original fixed-d shortening argument and gcd reduction do not handle arbitrary primitive length vectors, and the general word-equation bounds discussed in the supplied pack remain impractically large. The 120 remaining tuples through cap six include:

- The zero-head cases $i=0$, $k\le2\ell$ not already classified.
- Non-nested middle regions ($L<0$ or $Q<0$) with both outer exponents positive.
- The two strictly nested positive-outer rays with $k=1$, namely $i=2\ell$ and $i=2\ell+1$, after removing any already-known cases.

Boundary cases $L=0$ or $Q=0$ in the positive-outer region fall under the supplied balanced-prefix theorem, except its known nonforcing families. The explicit lists are authoritative for finite exponent caps.

The two k=1 rays are a particularly targeted next proof problem: here $L+Q=d$, so neither overlap is long enough to guarantee the primitive phase congruences by the present length argument. The zero-head non-nested region is another natural target. The phase/cancellation technique was chosen over an attempted global Euclidean descent because no valid descent for gcd-one length vectors was established. No blanket claim is made that the remaining cases have been ruled out.

## Provenance and priority

The conference problem and existing reductions come from the supplied manuscript. Its references include Š. Holub, *Periodicity forcing*, WORDS 2025 Open Problems; J. Hadravová and Š. Holub, *Equation x^iy^jx^k=u^iv^ju^k in words*, arXiv:1501.03602 (2015); and Š. Holub and J. Sýkora, *Binary equality words with two b's*, Comment. Math. Univ. Carolin. 59(2), 153–172 (2018). The latter concerns a different, two-b equality-word classification. The new arguments reuse standard Fine–Wilf, code and free-group facts and the supplied manuscript's stated theorems. They should be attributed accordingly. A comprehensive theorem-by-theorem literature priority check and an external mathematician's review have not been completed. Do not describe the new theorems as independently certified or the entire conjecture as solved.
